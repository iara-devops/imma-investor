The Opportunity

Building the AI operating layer for Brazil's 20M+ SMBs —
the world's largest WhatsApp-native business market.

Brazilian SMBs run their customer operations almost entirely on WhatsApp, where penetration reaches 99% (Statista). They lose customers to slow response times, burn staff hours on repetitive queries, and make financial decisions without reliable data. ImmA closes all three gaps with purpose-built AI agents, with Portugal and Iberia as the next geographic expansion.

The Problem

SMBs operating on WhatsApp face a structural inefficiency: customer interactions require constant human availability, but staffing 24/7 support is economically unviable at their scale. The result is lost leads after hours, staff overloaded with repetitive queries, and no reliable system for financial performance tracking. Existing chatbot solutions address only surface symptoms — they break when customers go off-script, and they hallucinate when they don't know the answer.

Primary Product

IARA

Intelligent Agent for Autonomous Resolutions. A context-aware AI agent deployed on WhatsApp that handles inbound customer interactions end-to-end — 24/7, across text, audio, image, and PDF.

  • Zero hallucination — responds only from client-approved knowledge bases
  • Context memory — recognises returning customers, retains full history
  • Graceful handoff to human agents with full conversation context
  • Automatic post-interaction CSAT collection via WhatsApp
  • Real-time team dashboard with queue management
Session-based pricing · No per-message billing · No service interruption on overage
Second Product

LucroMap

Profitability intelligence for SMBs with multi-product inventories. Transforms raw sales and cost data into actionable margin analysis — without requiring financial expertise from the owner.

  • 18 automated profitability indicators per product/SKU
  • ABC curve analysis — identifies which products actually drive profit
  • Revenue per m², per employee, per department
  • Flags low-margin production items before they compound
  • Premium tier: AI assistant for on-demand data queries
Natural upsell for IARA clients · High retention driver
